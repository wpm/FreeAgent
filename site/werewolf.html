Werewolf

A village, the werewolves hiding in it, and a vote every day.

A few of the players are secretly werewolves. The rest are villagers, among them a doctor and a seer. Play alternates between night and day, starting with night.

By night the werewolves, who know one another, choose a villager to kill, the doctor chooses one player to save, and the seer learns one player’s side. By day everyone talks and votes, and the player the village settles on is out. The villagers win when the last werewolf is dead. The werewolves win once they are as many as the villagers.

What it asks of agents

  • A day is one open conversation. Any player may speak at any moment, and every message reaches every living player as it’s said.
  • Votes stay live all day. A player can change a vote or take it back while the day is open. Whoever has the most votes is out once they have held the lead long enough, so timing and persuasion decide the day.
  • Night leaves secrets. The werewolves know each other, the seer knows what it has seen, and the doctor knows whom it saved. Each decides by day how much to reveal.
  • Every game has a winner, so every game is a result.

The roles

  • Werewolf

    Knows the other werewolves. Each night they kill a villager.

  • Villager

    Knows only their own role.

  • Seer

    Learns one player’s side each night.

  • Doctor

    Picks one player each night to save from the werewolves.

The table seats two werewolves, three villagers, a doctor and a seer, and gives each phase a minute. Change the counts and limits in a configuration file or in the app’s Settings.

Play it

In the desktop app, choose Play Werewolf. From the command line:

social-deduction play werewolf/prompted.toml

Download Social Deduction.

A sample in your browser

Play a sample game of Werewolf right in your browser. Press Play to watch it unfold, then step back through it move by move. It’s a random table: every player chooses at random, with no language model. Games played by models, your own tables and settings, and the command line are in the app.
